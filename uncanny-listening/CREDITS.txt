Uncanny Listening - credits and licences
========================================

Research: Ana Marjalizo, BA; PhD student in Aesthetic and Electronic Music and lecturer in training, Faculty of
  Information and Audiovisual Media, University of Barcelona (UB), Barcelona, Spain. Her research question, her
  classes of songs, her descriptors (dinámica, armonía, timbre, complejidad estructural) and her method (k-means
  aligned to her labels) are what this tool is built around.
Design: Miguel Angel Crozzoli. Implemented with Claude AI agents. Built with DarkSonification
  (https://github.com/Intelligent-Instruments-Lab/darksonification, Apache-2.0, © Miguel Angel Crozzoli and
  Intelligent Instrument Lab).

DATA
None is published. The page holds no audio and no data: each researcher opens her own folders, analysed in the
  browser; nothing is uploaded. The tests read the researcher's corpus in place on the developer's machine.

METHODS PORTED TO JAVASCRIPT (no library shipped; each tested against the original, see TESTS in the repository)
DarkLibrosa (DarkSonification), summary mode, as it computed the researcher's features in January 2026.
librosa 0.11 (ISC; McFee et al., "librosa: Audio and Music Signal Analysis in Python", SciPy 2015): STFT, mel
  filterbank (Slaney), power_to_db, MFCC (DCT-II), chroma_stft with estimate_tuning / piptrack, spectral centroid,
  bandwidth, rolloff, flatness, contrast (Jiang et al. 2002), onset strength (spectral flux), onset_detect
  (peak picking after Böck et al. 2012), feature.tempo (tempogram autocorrelation with a log-normal prior; Ellis
  2007), zero-crossing rate, rms.
Resampling to the libsoxr "HQ" specification (libsoxr, LGPL-2.1; used as a specification only): a Kaiser-windowed sinc.
FLAC decoding after the FLAC format specification (Xiph.Org; RFC 9639): our own decoder.
scikit-learn 1.7 (BSD-3-Clause): MinMaxScaler, PCA (svd_flip sign rule), KMeans (k-means++, Arthur & Vassilvitskii
  2007; Lloyd 1982), KernelDensity, LogisticRegression (L2, fitted by L-BFGS: Liu & Nocedal 1989), adjusted Rand
  index (Hubert & Arabie 1985).
numpy (BSD-3-Clause): the legacy RandomState stream, Mersenne Twister MT19937 (Matsumoto & Nishimura 1998), so the
  clustering draws the same numbers as the desktop.
DarkSonification's align_labels: flip for two labels; Hungarian assignment otherwise (Kuhn 1955; Munkres 1957).
Effect sizes: Cohen's d (pooled SD); η² for more classes. Boundary field: Nadaraya–Watson kernel regression
  (Nadaraya 1964; Watson 1964).
